An Emerging Scientific Platform

Extending Healthy Life for Every Animal

Veterinary Longevity is an emerging scientific and educational platform founded in Lisbon, dedicated to advancing the understanding of aging, healthspan, and preventive veterinary care in animals.

Currently in development, we are building a global network of clinicians, researchers, and partners committed to advancing animal healthspan.

Core Research Areas

What We Study

Our work spans comparative gerontology, clinical veterinary medicine, population genetics, and disease epidemiology — four disciplines that inform and strengthen one another.

Aging & Longevity Science

Investigating the molecular and cellular mechanisms that govern lifespan across species, from companion animals to exotic wildlife and livestock.

Clinical Veterinary Support

We are building evidence-based longevity protocols, tools, and educational resources to support veterinarians and clinic teams in extending animal healthspan.

Disease Biomarkers

Identifying early biological indicators of age-related disease in animals, enabling earlier intervention and significantly improved long-term health outcomes.

Population Health

We aim to aggregate longitudinal health data from partner clinics to reveal population-level trends in animal aging, mortality, and chronic disease.

Founded by a Clinician

Dr. Soheyl Simaei

Veterinarian 20+ Years Clinical Practice Lisbon, Portugal

Veterinary Longevity was founded by Dr. Soheyl Simaei, a veterinarian and clinical entrepreneur with more than 20 years of experience in veterinary practice, clinic leadership, and professional education. Based in Lisbon, Dr. Soheyl is developing Veterinary Longevity to bridge real-world clinical practice with the emerging science of aging, prevention, and healthspan medicine in animals.
