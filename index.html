Core Research Areas

What We Study

Our multidisciplinary approach spans comparative gerontology, clinical veterinary medicine, population genetics, and disease epidemiology.

Aging & Longevity Science

Investigating the molecular and cellular mechanisms that govern lifespan across species — from companion animals to exotic wildlife and livestock.

Clinical Veterinary Support

Providing practicing veterinarians and clinic teams with evidence-based longevity protocols, tools, and educational resources to raise the standard of care.

Disease Biomarkers

Identifying early biological indicators of age-related disease in animals, enabling earlier intervention and significantly improved long-term health outcomes.

Population Health

Analyzing large-scale longitudinal health data from enrolled clinics to reveal population-level trends in animal aging, mortality, and chronic disease.
